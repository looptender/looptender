- The panel page lists the events it is told about (`notices`, the same list the macOS menu reads) in a Notifications area on the Overview: the last ten with unread marks and a count in the page title, notices that begin together as one line, and a choice of kinds. Browser notifications are made only after you click **Notify me** and allow them; nothing leaves the computer, and the page stores nothing in the browser, so choices last until a reload.
