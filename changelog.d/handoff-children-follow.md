- After a handoff to a BB thread looptender created (`bb.new_thread`), the direct child threads of the conversation left are moved to the created thread with `bb thread update --parent-thread`, and back on an automatic switch-back, so their completions wake a conversation that can act. Only the parent changes; a child in error stays in error on its own provider. At most 50 per handoff, each update confirmed by listing the left thread again and retried at most 3 times; recorded in `state.json` (`childMove`) so a crash resumes with the children still to move; a stop, pause or other hold ends the moves. `status` shows the counts. Opt out per candidate with `bb.move_children: false`.
