- `looptender archive -c CONFIG [--note TEXT]` and `unarchive` put a loop that is not supervising out of the way and back. An archived loop is listed apart (state `archived`, counted in `archived`, `panel list --archived`), needs nobody, gets no resume or group pause/stop, cannot be resumed, enabled or started until unarchived, and its daemon exits cleanly at its next check. Nothing else changes and unarchive resumes nothing. The record is `archive.json` beside `control.json`, so builds from before it see the loop as held and the state format is unchanged. Every JSON surface carries `naming`: one display name per line of work (`display-name.json`, set with `looptender display-name` or `init --display-name`, or the BB thread's title at the first check), with `shortName`, `slug`, `project` and `titleDiffers`.
