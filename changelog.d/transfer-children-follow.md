- `transfer apply` now moves the source thread's direct, non-archived child threads to the destination once the transfer has finished, with the same mechanism a handoff to a created thread uses: at most 50, each confirmed by listing both threads, retried at most 3 times, recorded in `state.json` with the transfer's identity so `transfer recover --id` can finish it (and is a no-op once done), and ended by any control write after the transfer. Only a child's parent changes; the loop stays held for a person. `--leave-children` keeps them where they are. `transfer preview` says how many would move and how many run on a provider other than the destination's, and the "moved" note says how many moved; counts only, no child IDs. `transfer supersede` moves nothing. The adoption state format rises to 5 (`childMove` gained `transfer` and `left`).
