- A stop now also reaches a successor lane whose creation was asked of bb and not recorded (the supervisor stopped between `bb thread spawn` and saving the answer): the stop check looks it up by its title and stops it, settling nothing, as it does for a created thread. Before, such a lane kept working on a blocked lane's task after a stop. The successor pass after a handoff now judges a lane's reported reset by the check's own time, as the session's observation does, instead of the wall clock, so a check run with `tick --now` replaces the same lanes a live check would. Both were found by end-to-end scenarios of the incident on a loop armed with `fallbacks arm`.
