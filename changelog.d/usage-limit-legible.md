- At a provider usage limit, looptender now says what is limited and what you can do. It keeps the window BB names in its rate-limit event (5-hour, weekly for all models, or one model's week) with its reset. It names the accounts catalog entry that reached the limit (the one the route states, or the one for the Claude directory the check ran under) and the other configured accounts with their last quota reading. It gives two fixed-wording actions: the one command that logs BB's Claude directory in to another account, with what that restarts, and, when no takeover is armed, `looptender fallbacks arm -c CONFIG` to move the work to another provider. This shows in `status`, `status --json`, client status, the panel and `panel list` (placeholders only, no paths). A coordinator's child-error wake adds one line counting its lanes at a usage limit, with the window and the reset. `state.json` gains `usageLimit`, so the daemon's adoption state format rises to 7.
