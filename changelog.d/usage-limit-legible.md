- At a provider usage limit, looptender now says what is limited and what you can do. It keeps the window BB names in its rate-limit event (5-hour, weekly for all models, or one model's week) with its reset. It names the accounts catalog entry that reached the limit when the route states it; otherwise it names, as "probably" and with why it may be wrong, the entry for the Claude directory the supervisor was started with, since BB does not say which directory a thread runs under. It lists the other configured accounts with their last quota reading. It gives two fixed-wording actions: the one command that logs BB's Claude directory in to another account, with what that restarts, and, when no takeover is armed, `looptender fallbacks arm -c CONFIG` to move the work to another provider. This shows in `status`, `status --json`, client status (accounts by handle, unless `--private`), the panel and `panel list` (codes per loop, with the words once per list in `usageLimitWords`; no paths or labels). With lane recovery on, a coordinator's child-error wake adds one line counting the inspected lanes at a usage limit, with the window and the reset; nothing reads a lane for it. The limit is kept in its own record, `usage-limit.json` in the state directory, not in `state.json`: the adoption state format is unchanged, and an older build neither reads nor rewrites it.
