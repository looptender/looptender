- **On by default:** after a handoff to a BB thread looptender created, the takeover now also starts a successor thread on the new provider for each child lane that was blocked mid-work, as a child lane of the new coordinator. A blocked lane is one in error with a usage limit, or with a blocked window and a future reset, on another provider; a BB thread cannot change provider. Turn it off per fallback with `bb.replace_blocked_children: false`. **Duplicate work is possible:** the old lane is left as it was, and when its provider's limit resets BB may retry it by itself while the successor works. looptender never retries a replaced lane and never replaces one BB reports as retrying, and the successor is told to check the old lane first; stopping the old lane is a person's call.
  - Successors use `bb.child_model` and `bb.child_reasoning` (default: the candidate's own) and `bb.child_permission_mode` (default `accept-edits`). Their permission mode is never above the lane's own or the coordinator's, and is `accept-edits` when BB does not report the lane's mode.
  - Each successor is parented to the created thread and starts with a fixed prompt ending `Continue from @thread:<old lane>.`.
  - It runs once per handoff, only for the lanes that handoff moved. `status`, `doctor`, `init` and the `fallbacks add` preview say beforehand, for each such fallback, what a handoff will start and how to turn it off.
  - A creation that provably never happened is cleared without a hold. `transfer replace-children --discard-pending-successor` forgets one BB can never settle; find and stop that thread first.
  - Idle and finished lanes are left for the new coordinator. Its first prompt gives the expected numbers of successors started and blocked lanes skipped at the cap; `status` gives the actual ones.
  - At most 5 per handoff, and `loop.limits.max_child_successors` over the loop's life (default 10, at most 50). Each creation is also a send against `max_sends`. Creation follows the new-thread rules: the intent is saved and counted first, and is found by title after any failure; an unknown outcome holds the loop for a person.
  - After a manual transfer, a person runs `transfer replace-children`: `--dry-run` previews, `--model` is required, `--all-old-provider` widens the selection. It uses the lifetime cap but not `max_sends`.
  - `status` counts successors and lanes skipped at each cap, with no lane IDs or titles. The adoption state format rises to 6.
