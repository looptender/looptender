- A line of work that moves to a new loop keeps one name and one row: `looptender archive -c OLD --succeeded-by NEW_CONFIG`, `init --succeeds OLD_CONFIG`, or `transfer supersede` naming the successor's thread records that the new loop continues the old one. The new loop takes the old one's display name at once, and its first completed check archives the old loop if it is held then, with a note naming its successor; until then neither changes. `status --json` carries `continuedFrom` and the whole `lineage`; lists carry only `lineage: {count, from}` and the successor's key on an archived loop.
