- **Default change for new BB loops:** when BB reports another usable provider, `init`'s wizard now offers a takeover by default (yes unless you answer no): one `bb.new_thread` fallback on that provider, with handoff on for rate limits, so a usage limit starts a new conversation there that owns the loop, takes this conversation's child lanes, and starts successors for lanes blocked by the limit. "Usable" is read from metadata only: `bb provider list --json` says available, it is `claude-code` or `codex` and not the loop's own provider, its local CLI is on `PATH`, and `bb provider models` names a default model; no login is checked and no model is called. Scripts are unchanged unless they ask: `--new-thread-provider P --new-thread-model M` (optionally `--new-thread-reasoning`, `--new-thread-permission-mode`) sets it up, `--new-thread-provider none` declines it, and without them a usable provider is only named (`Takeover: not set up`, `newThreadOffer` in `--json`). The preview says exactly what happens at a limit. Existing loops are not changed by an update: `keepgoing fallbacks arm -c <config>` adds the same fallback after a preview, from the loop's owner or a terminal, and doctor's single-route warning names it. The BB plugin's **Add to Looptender** preview includes the takeover by default, with a checkbox to turn it off. Headless loops are unchanged.
