- A full disk no longer goes unnoticed, and cannot corrupt a loop's records. `doctor` (`state.disk_space`) and `status` (`disk:`, `diskSpace`) warn when the state directory's volume has less than the larger of 1 GiB and 2% free, and report an error below 100 MiB; a daemon records each worsening once in `runs.jsonl` and sends one `error` report. `runs.jsonl` now gets whole lines only, a headless target's saved session ID and turn markers are replaced whole instead of rewritten in place, a move of child threads waits until its record is saved, and build adoption waits while the volume is nearly full. See [a full disk](docs/operations.md#a-full-disk).
