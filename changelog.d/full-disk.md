- A full disk no longer goes unnoticed, and cannot corrupt a loop's records. `doctor` (`state.disk_space`) and `status` (`disk:`, `diskSpace`) warn when the state directory's volume has less than the larger of 1 GiB and 2% free, and report an error below 100 MiB; a daemon records each level once per episode in `runs.jsonl` and sends one `error` report for it, with a recovery margin and at most one report an hour, so free space hovering at a threshold does not repeat it. `runs.jsonl` and the routes history now get whole lines only, under a lock all appenders take; a headless target's saved session ID and turn markers are replaced whole instead of rewritten in place; a move of child threads waits until its record is saved; and build adoption waits, and says so once, while the volume is nearly full. See [a full disk](docs/operations.md#a-full-disk).
