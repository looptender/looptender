- The macOS menu and the panel page share their notification settings: one file in the panel directory keeps which kinds are on and the browser opt-in (the page saves a choice through a new `POST api/v1/notify`, available on a read-only panel; `looptender panel notify set|heartbeat` is the menu's way to write it), so a choice made in one is the other's and a reload keeps them. The menu records that it runs and notifies; while it does, the panel page does not send browser notifications for the same events and says why beside **Notify me**. A menu started after being down tells once about an urgent question that began meanwhile. The panel's list opens when something new arrives and is never closed for you, a browser notification focuses the loop when clicked, and a group is told by its real total.
