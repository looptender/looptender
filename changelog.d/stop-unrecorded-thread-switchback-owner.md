- A stop now reaches a thread created for a handoff whose outcome was not yet recorded, as when the supervisor stopped between `bb thread spawn` and saving the answer. The stop check looks the thread up by its title, read-only, and stops every thread listed under it. It settles nothing under the stop: the target, owner and children stay put, and the creation is settled after `resume`. When none is listed, the tick output says to stop it by hand. Before, the stop went only to the conversation being left, and the created thread kept working. An automatic switch-back now saves its ownership move before its brief. After a supervisor killed right after the brief, the documented recovery (`retarget primary`, then `resume`) returns ownership to the primary's thread. Before, ownership stayed with the created thread, which could still resume and enable the loop while the primary's commands were refused. See [hand off to a new conversation](docs/fallbacks.md#hand-off-to-a-new-conversation).
