- `looptender wait --until host-session|network|time:RFC3339|limit-reset [--for DURATION]`: the loop sends nothing until a condition the supervisor checks itself holds, then carries on by itself with no resume; after `--for` (default 24 hours) it becomes an ordinary await-user. It is saved as an await-user hold, so older builds send nothing either, and shown as its own state (`waiting_on_condition`, `waiting.kind: condition`). `looptender await-user --withdraw --note WHY` lets the loop's owner thread take back the await-user it set itself, only if nothing changed since and the history shows it; it restores exactly the record from before, so a person's hold stays.
