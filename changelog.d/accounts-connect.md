- `looptender accounts connect --provider claude-code|codex --label NAME` connects another subscription in one step: it makes a new private directory, runs the provider's own sign-in in it and adds it to the account catalog. looptender does not see or copy the login. `accounts disconnect` takes an entry out of the catalog and prints the provider's sign-out command; it signs nothing out and deletes nothing. A second Antigravity account is refused: Antigravity keeps one login per computer. Whether a new login is a different account from those already connected is not checked yet.
