- Supervision gaps are no longer silent. An enabled loop that is not held and has no recorded check (as after a move or transfer, which clears the check record) is judged from when it was enabled, resumed or moved, and after 15 minutes (or three daemon intervals) with nothing checking it, `status`, `doctor` (as an error), the panel's needs, the macOS menu, the BB plugin card and `bb looptender status` say that nothing is supervising it; held, complete and disabled loops never are. A loop checked by ticks without a daemon says so, with the cadence of its recent checks, instead of "next check unknown". A supervisor start refused for ownership or a pending transfer is recorded in `runs.jsonl` and shown by `status` and `doctor` until a supervisor starts, without changing control. `status` and `doctor` name the build of the last check, of the command and of the daemon, and say whether the daemon will follow new installs. See [external schedulers can stop on their own](docs/operations.md#external-schedulers-can-stop-on-their-own).
