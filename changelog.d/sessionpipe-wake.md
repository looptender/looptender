- An observed sessionpipe target can opt in to waking its session when it is idle (`sessionpipe.send: wake_only`, with a `loop.prompt`). The wake runs `sessionpipe ask <harness>:<id> --quiet --text -` detached, with the answer sent to the null device unread. A refusal within 3 s counts as not sent and keeps only its code. One wake is outstanding at a time. Nudges, re-prompts, stops, handoffs and switch-backs stay held; send limits, idle spacing and every hold apply; nothing is sent while the session waits on a person or this computer's login session is unavailable. Doctor checks that asking is on and sessionpipe's service is running. Off by default; fixture-tested with a stand-in sessionpipe.
