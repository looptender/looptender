- The panel's report and `panel list --json` now say, for every loop, what it waits for and who set it, so the menu and the page need not guess from the words. `waiting.kind` is `asked` (its own agent asked for a person), `held` (a person or another thread held it), `limit` (a check reached a limit or deadline), `checks` or `unknown`; `by` comes from the incident journal's record of who saved the hold. Each row also carries a one-line `reason` in fixed words, `checkedAt` (when the facts were read by its last check) and `supervisor` (whether a daemon answered just now). A loop is no longer shown as "Supervising" while the daemon recorded for it does not answer. The advice for a hold with a note no longer says only "resume when done": a retired loop's note asks for a stop. The overview says how many supervisors answered.
