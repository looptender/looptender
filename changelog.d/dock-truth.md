- The panel's report and `panel list --json` now say, for every loop, what it waits for and who set it, so the menu and the page need not guess from the words. `waiting.kind` is `asked` (its own thread or a lane asked for a person), `held` (a hold saved by a control command), `limit` (a check reached a limit or deadline), `checks`, `unreadable` or `unknown`; `by` is the role the incident journal records for whoever saved the hold (`owner_thread`, `other_thread`, `terminal`, `client`), or `check` or `unknown`. Each row also carries a one-line `reason` in fixed words, `checkedAt` (when the facts were read by its last check) and `supervisor` (whether a daemon answered just now). A loop is no longer shown as "Supervising" while the daemon recorded for it does not answer when asked twice; the daemon question is now retried once after a miss, for `status` too. The advice for a hold with a note no longer says only "resume when done": a retired loop's note asks for a stop. The overview says how many supervisors answered.
