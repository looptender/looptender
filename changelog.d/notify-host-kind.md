- The `host` notification kind (this computer's session or load, which belongs to no loop) can be switched in the macOS menu's **Notify me when…** and in the panel page, and is on by default, so a login session or network that background processes lost, or an overloaded computer, is told like a usage limit is. A menu that quits no longer writes its "not notifying" over the beat of the menu that replaced it: the heartbeat names its writer, and a late retraction from another writer is ignored.
